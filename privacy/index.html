Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Kor is built so that your tax figures never leave your phone. This policy says what that means in practice.

What Kor stores

The figures you enter: your profile, salary, other income, investments, tax already paid and wealth statement. They are saved on this phone only, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Kor also keeps a few preferences, such as your reminder and theme choices.

What Kor does not do

Kor has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We never receive your figures and have no way to see them.

Backups and new phones

Kor excludes its data from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. If you change phones, your figures do not move with you automatically. Uninstalling Kor or clearing its data deletes them for good.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are off until you turn them on. If you do, Kor asks for notification permission and schedules reminders on the phone. They name the deadline but never show an amount.

Copying and saving

When you tap a line on the e-return summary, Kor copies its amount to the clipboard, marked private on Android 13 and later. On older versions other apps may be able to read the clipboard. Save as PDF writes a file only where you choose, through Android's file picker. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Links and email

The Website and Contact rows open your browser or mail app. Kor itself sends nothing. Anything you email us is seen by us and handled by your mail provider, so please do not include real tax figures or identifiers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new text ships with the app update and the date above changes. Any change that adds network access would be recorded in the changelog and README first. Questions: kor@azimulkabir.com, or kor.azimulkabir.com.

ব্যাকআপ ও নতুন ফোন

কর তার তথ্য অ্যান্ড্রয়েড ব্যাকআপ ও ডিভাইস থেকে ডিভাইসে স্থানান্তরের বাইরে রাখে। ফোন বদলালে আপনার হিসাব নিজে থেকে সঙ্গে যাবে না। কর আনইনস্টল করলে বা এর ডেটা মুছলে হিসাব চিরতরে মুছে যায়।

নোটিফিকেশন

আপনি চালু না করা পর্যন্ত সময়সীমার রিমাইন্ডার বন্ধ থাকে। চালু করলে কর নোটিফিকেশনের অনুমতি চায় এবং ফোনেই রিমাইন্ডার নির্ধারণ করে। রিমাইন্ডারে সময়সীমার কথা থাকে, কিন্তু কোনো অঙ্ক দেখানো হয় না।

কপি ও সংরক্ষণ
